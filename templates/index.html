    How to play

    Every day you get 5 real headlines from the day before. In each one, a name (a person, place, country or company) is blacked out.

    • You have 20 seconds per headline. Guess as many times as you like.
    • You see the first letter and how long the word is. At 10 seconds, you get an extra hint.
    • A correct answer scores 100 points plus a speed bonus of up to 200.

    under 7s got it missed

    At the end, catch up on each story with links to the full articles and see how you did against everyone else. New headlines arrive at midnight, your time.
